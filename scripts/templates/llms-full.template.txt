## What Is Sintered Stone?

Sintered stone (sinterovaný kameň) is a premium surface material made from 100% natural minerals — clay, feldspar, silica, and natural pigments — compressed under 15,000 tonnes of pressure and fired at 1,200°C for 45 minutes. No resins, no artificial binders. The result is an ultra-dense, ultra-hard surface that mimics the appearance of natural stone (marble, travertine, concrete) while dramatically outperforming it in durability.

**Technical properties:**
- Porosity: < 0.1% (near-zero — stain-proof without sealing)
- Mohs hardness: 7+ (diamond is 10, granite is 6–7)
- Heat resistance: up to 300°C continuous, 500°C short-term
- UV resistance: no fading outdoors or in bright light
- Flexural strength: up to 16,000N for 20mm slabs
- Chemical resistance: resistant to acids (lemon, vinegar), alkalis, solvents
- Frost resistance: suitable for exterior applications
- Food safety: certified for direct food contact

**Thicknesses that exist on the market** (Orostone sells 12 mm only):
- 3mm: wall cladding, furniture fronts, overlay
- 6mm: lightweight applications, furniture
- 12mm: standard countertops, floors, walls
- 20mm: premium worktops, outdoor, high-load floors

**Slab sizes on the market:** 3200×1600mm, 3200×1440mm (varies by design). Orostone sells 3200×1600mm.

**Finishes available:**
- Matte (matný): softer appearance, hides fingerprints, feels silky
- Polished (leštený): mirror-like, dramatic veining, shows fingerprints more
- Leather/Textured (štruktúrovaný): tactile surface, non-slip

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Product FAQs

**Q: Is sintered stone suitable for bathrooms?**
A: Yes. Porcelain large-format slabs have near-zero porosity (< 0.1%), making them ideal for bathrooms, showers, and wellness spaces. No sealing required. They withstand permanent water contact without damage.

**Q: How do I care for the surface?**
A: Maintenance is minimal — warm water and mild soap is sufficient. The material is non-porous, requires no sealing or waxing. It resists common cleaning products including mild acids (lemon, vinegar).

**Q: Can I place a hot pot directly on the surface?**
A: Yes. Sintered stone withstands temperatures up to 300°C. You can place hot pots or pans directly on the surface without risk of damage or discolouration.

**Q: What is the delivery time?**
A: Stock slabs are typically delivered in 3–5 working days from the order; slabs to order ship in 3–6 weeks depending on availability at the manufacturer. The whole kitchen project, from consultation to finished kitchen, takes 10–15 working days.

**Q: Is sintered stone the same as Dekton or Neolith?**

## The Orostone Process — 10 Steps

Orostone advises, supplies the material (whole slabs) and coordinates the schedule. Measuring, fabrication, transport and installation are done and invoiced by the partner stonemason. Full guide: https://orostone.sk/blog/od-merania-po-instalaciu-proces-orostone

1. **Consultation and material selection** — Free consultation at the Bošany showroom, where you compare full slabs, not just small samples.
2. **Quote** — Within 48 hours of the consultation: an itemised quote with the material from Orostone and measuring, fabrication, transport and installation from the partner stonemason.
3. **Measuring** — After quote approval and the deposit, the partner stonemason who will fabricate the worktop measures your kitchen by hand on site. Cabinets must be installed and levelled. The stonemason prepares drawings; fabrication starts only after you approve them.
4. **CNC fabrication** — At the partner stonemason's workshop: diamond-tool cutting, cutouts and edge profiles. 7–10 working days from drawing approval.
5. **Quality control** — The stonemason inspects every piece for thickness, surface and edge defects before it leaves the workshop.
6. **Transport** — The stonemason transports the finished pieces upright in A-frames.
7. **Substrate preparation** — Cabinet tops are checked for level and shimmed where needed.
8. **Installation** — Carried out by the partner stonemason experienced with sintered stone.
9. **Appliances** — The stonemason fits the sink, hob and tap during installation; electrical and gas connections are done by a qualified electrician or gas technician.
10. **Final inspection and handover** — Joint and level checks, cleaning, care instructions. Warranty: 24 months on the material; the stonemason gives the warranty on fabrication and installation.

Total project timeline: 10–15 working days from first consultation to finished kitchen.
