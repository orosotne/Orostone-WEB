# OROSTONE — Premium Sintered Stone Surfaces

> OROSTONE is a Slovak premium supplier of sintered stone (sinterovaný kameň) for kitchen countertops, bathroom vanities, wall cladding, and architectural projects. Based in Bratislava, Slovakia. We provide end-to-end service: expert consultation, digital laser templating (±0.05mm precision), CNC diamond fabrication, A-frame transport, and professional installation carried out by partner stonemasons experienced with large-format slabs. All projects completed in 10–15 working days.

## Key Facts

- Sintered stone is 100% mineral material sintered at 1,200°C without resins
- Withstands temperatures up to 300°C, UV radiation, acids, and scratches
- Near-zero porosity (under 0.1%) — stain-proof without sealing
- Orostone catalog: 12 mm slabs in 3200 × 1600 mm format (the material itself also exists in 3 mm and 20 mm variants)
- Mohs hardness 6–7, breaking force up to 16,000N for 20mm slabs
- Popular finishes: matte, polished, leather/textured
