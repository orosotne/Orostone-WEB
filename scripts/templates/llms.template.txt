# Orostone — Premium Sintered Stone Surfaces

> Orostone is a Slovak premium supplier of sintered stone (sinterovaný kameň) for kitchen countertops, kitchen islands, backsplashes, bathrooms, and wall cladding. Registered office in Bratislava, showroom with full slabs in Bošany, Slovakia. We help you choose a decor, sell whole slabs (3200 × 1600 × 12 mm), deliver them across Slovakia, and coordinate the project. Measuring (by hand, on site), fabrication, transport of the finished pieces, and installation are done and invoiced by a partner stonemason — one we recommend or one you choose.

## Key Facts

- Sintered stone is 100% mineral material sintered at 1,200°C without resins
- Withstands temperatures up to 300°C, UV radiation, acids, and scratches
- Near-zero porosity (under 0.1%) — stain-proof without sealing
- Orostone catalog: 12 mm slabs in 3200 × 1600 mm format (the material itself also exists in 3 mm and 20 mm variants)
- Mohs hardness 7+, breaking force up to 16,000N for 20mm slabs
- Popular finishes: matte, polished, leather/textured

- Fabrication and installation: the partner stonemason's orientation rate is ≈ {{INSTALLATION_RATE}} €/m² incl. VAT (measuring, transport, edge processing, polishing, installation). The stonemason invoices this work; it is not part of Orostone's material price.
- Samples (10 × 10 cm): the first sample is free, each further sample costs 4.90 € and shipping 2.50 € (incl. VAT), max. three samples per order
- Liability for defects: the material carries the 24-month statutory liability for defects; the stonemason gives the warranty on fabrication and installation
- Delivery: Slovakia only; stock slabs typically within 3–5 working days
